Privacy Policy

Windover for macOS · Effective September 29, 2026

In short: Windover does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything it captures stays on your Mac.

Screen recording permission

Windover asks for macOS's Screen Recording permission so it can capture the region of the screen you select. A capture happens only when you start one yourself from the menu bar icon, and only the area you select is captured.

Your captures

  • Captured images are kept in memory and shown only in the floating window on your Mac.
  • They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • Closing a floating window discards its image.
  • An image is written to disk only when you choose Save and pick a location, and copied to the clipboard only when you choose Copy.

Data collection

Windover has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and it contains no third-party SDKs. The app does not make network connections.

Children

Windover does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
